About Company - Globe Civil Projects

Globe Civil Project is an integrated engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) company. The company is engaged in infrastructure projects, like Transport and logistics projects, social and commercial projects, and non-infrastructure projects like commercial offices and housing.

The company has also diversified into specialized infrastructure and non-infrastructure projects like railway bridges, airport terminal, elevated railway terminal, and railway bridges and hospitals.

Additionally, the company also provides mechanical, electrical, and plumbing, architectural and structural work, etc

It is also a registered Class-1 Super contractor with the Central Public Work Department (“CPWD”), Government of India to tender for any work up to Rs 650 crore.
