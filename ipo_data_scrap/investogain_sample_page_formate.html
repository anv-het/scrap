About Company - AJC Jewel

AJC Jewel manufacturers is engaged in the business of manfuacturing and designing a range of wholesale gold jewelleries. It offers extensive range of gold jewellery designs in 22karat and 18 karat , like plain gold, rose gold, jewelleries studded with Cubic Zircon and/or coloured stones.

Its product portfoio includes Anklet, Bangle, Bracelet, Earrings, Necklace, Nose Pin, Pendant, Ring and customized named jewelleries (like Name Bracelet, Name Pendant, Name Ring) etc. based on customers preferences. The products are sold to local dealers, showrooms and small-scale jewellery shops in bulk.

Its gold jewellery manufacturing facility is located at Inkel Greens Edu City, Malappuram across 21,780.76 Sq. Ft. The facility is equipped with necessary equipments like 3D printer, Wax Injector, Casting Machine, and polishing equipment and other handling equipment.
