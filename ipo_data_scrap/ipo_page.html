About Company - Indogulf Cropsciences

Indogulf Cropsciences Ltd is manufactures crop protection products, plant nutrients, and biologicals. The company manufactures and markets an extensive range of products such as water dispersible granules (“WDG”), suspension concentrate (“SC”), capsule suspension (“CS”), ultra low volume (“ULV”), emulsion in water (“EW”), soluble granule (“SG”), flowable suspension (“FS”), etc.

The products offered by the company are used for various crops, including cereals, pulses and oilseeds, fibre crops, plantations, and fruits and vegetables.

It served both domestic and international customers, such as Krishi Rasayan Exports Private Limited, Delhi, Parijat Industries (India) Private Limited, Delhi, BR Agrotech Private Limited, Delhi, Crystal Crop Protection Limited, Delhi, and Asasiat of Development for Agric & Trade Co., UAE.

Currently, the company operates four manufacturing facilities in Samba, Jammu and Kashmir; (ii) Nathupur - I, Haryana; (iii) Nathupur - II, Haryana; and (iv) Barwasni, Haryana across 20 acres of land. Its manufacturing facility is certified with ISO 9001: 2015 and ISO 14001: 2015.
